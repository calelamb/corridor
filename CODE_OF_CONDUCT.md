# Community conduct

Treat contributors and people affected by road and wildlife decisions with
respect. Welcome questions, credit evidence and explain disagreements without
personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing and
publication of private information are not acceptable.

Protect wildlife locations and contributor privacy. Do not share sensitive
records in public discussion, even as examples. Maintainers may remove harmful
content, limit participation or exclude repeated or severe violations.

Raise conduct concerns privately with the repository owner using the hosting
provider's private reporting channel when configured. Include only the
information needed to assess the issue. Maintainers should protect reporter
confidentiality, consider the context, and communicate decisions fairly.
