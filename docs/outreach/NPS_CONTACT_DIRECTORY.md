## 1. Directory Overview & Operational Protocol

This directory provides verified public contact structures, headquarters coordinates, divisional hierarchies, phone protocols, and regional liaison offices for eight flagship National Park Service (NPS) units prioritized for Corridor deployment under the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) Wildlife Crossings Pilot Program (WCPP, 23 U.S.C. § 171).

Federal civil servants, regional transportation planners, and Corridor outreach specialists must follow standard Department of the Interior (DOI) and federal outreach protocols:
1. **Respect Division Chains of Command:** Address correspondence according to operational scope. Coordinate biological science through the **Division of Resource Management & Science**, field patrol and incident reporting through the **Division of Visitor and Resource Protection**, and roadway maintenance or structural engineering through **Facility Management & Transportation**.
2. **Adhere to Non-Placeholder Email Guidelines:** Government security gateways reject mass automated campaigns and flag unsolicited communications. Outreach specialists must verify active incumbent assignments via switchboard protocols before dispatching personalized briefings.
3. **Multi-Agency Alignment:** Effective highway wildlife mitigation requires tripartite coordination between NPS park units, FHWA Division/Federal Lands Highway offices, and State DOT wildlife crossing coordinators.

### 2.1 Department of the Interior / National Park Service Email Conventions
The National Park Service operates under standardized Department of the Interior Enterprise Directory Services (EDS). Official individual email addresses follow these strict conventions:
- **Primary Standard:** `first_last@nps.gov` (e.g., `jane_doe@nps.gov`)
- **Middle Initial Disambiguation:** `first_m_last@nps.gov` (e.g., `jane_m_doe@nps.gov`)
- **Suffix Disambiguation:** `first_last_1@nps.gov` (used when multiple civil servants share identical names)

### 2.3 Non-Placeholder Verification Rules for Outreach Specialists
> [!IMPORTANT]
> - **Zero Placeholder Permitted:** Never send outreach messages to generic placeholders (`[email protected]`, `info@nps.gov`, `webmaster@nps.gov`) or unverified web-scraped lists.
> - **Switchboard Verification:** Call the park administrative switchboard prior to first outbound contact to confirm the current incumbent's full name, exact title, and preferred division routing.
> - **Anti-Spam & FOIA Compliance:** All communications to federal and state government accounts are subject to public disclosure under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA, 5 U.S.C. § 552) or state open records statutes. Avoid commercial language, tracking pixels, or sales terminology.

### 6.2 Pre-Flight & Post-Outreach Checklist
Prior to sending any communication or logging an interaction in the tracking matrix:

- [ ] **Incumbent Identity Checked:** Verified active duty status and exact spelling via telephone switchboard or official park press contacts.
- [ ] **Address & Routing Verified:** Confirmed appropriate division (RMS vs. VRP vs. Facility Management) to ensure correspondence does not cross operational boundaries.
- [ ] **Template Customization Complete:** All placeholder variables (`[Park Name]`, `[Target Species]`, `[Highway]`) replaced with unit-specific data.
- [ ] **Zero-Cost & Open-Source Stated:** Explicitly reiterated Apache-2.0 license, free public access, and absence of procurement hurdles.
- [ ] **GFE / PWA Capability Emphasized:** Highlighted that the PWA runs in standard Edge/Chrome with zero IT installation privileges.
- [ ] **Dual-Resolution Privacy Highlighted:** For parks with sensitive/listed species (e.g., grizzly bear, Sierra Nevada bighorn sheep), verified that H3 resolution-6 30-day quarantine protections are detailed.
- [ ] **Follow-up Calendar Updated:** Respectful 10–14 business day follow-up window logged in the tracking registry.
