# Collision and carcass data sweep — 2026-09-21

Research-only catalog for Corridor. Checked live official government, university and author-repository sources on **2026-09-21 UTC**. This report covers western states, national fatal crashes, and park mortality. It does not establish operational coverage. **Production ingestion: zero.** No access requests, accounts, payments, agreements or messages to custodians were submitted.

The 24 entries below include independent datasets, derivative products, request-only datasets and one explicitly labeled report-only lead; they are not 24 cleared event datasets. Five complete dataset artifacts from four source families were downloaded privately: FARS, two Utah model tables, Montana carcasses, and Craters of the Moon. FARS and the two Utah tables have affirmative reuse policies; Montana transformation rights and the park workbook's exact authorship/rights still need resolution. All other **dataset retrieval timestamps and dataset SHA-256 values are null**. Downloaded metadata, manuals and reports are evidence, not dataset acquisitions.

## Findings that change the prior inventory

- **Montana now publishes statewide carcasses directly.** The current MDT page links 2016–2025 carcass and crash/vehicle/person workbooks. The downloaded carcass workbook contains 60,592 nonblank records; this is not a deduplicated collision count. MDT's website policy permits unmodified personal/informational copying and distribution, which does not clearly authorize Corridor's transformed, public database. Preserve that distinction.
- **Gallatin licensing is more explicit than previously recorded.** ROSA P's JSON states unrestricted access/use and sharing/copying/redistribution. The alternate ScholarWorks item page reveals exact CSV/README bitstreams. Actual bitstream downloads remain unsuccessful in this environment, so schema and file rights are not fully verified.
- **CROS specifies CC BY 3.0 US.** The support page's actual license link resolves to that license. Bulk export availability, contributor attribution and coverage still require work; its own-record CSV/JSON export is not a general bulk download.
- **Utah has public research derivatives.** Two downloaded, CC BY 4.0 mortality model tables offer real segment-season-year outcomes and covariates. They are binary outcomes, not individual carcass events. The authors expressly distinguish protected raw collar locations requiring GRAMA.
- **Idaho has a public raw roadkill service**, but general IDFG terms sharply restrict copying/public display while its GIS item gives only liability language. Do not infer a broad open license from the service's “open data” description.
- **NPS Craters of the Moon has an actual public workbook.** Its filename says 2010–2025, while a full-history worksheet starts in 1963. A catalog end date differs from dates inside the workbook. Multiple sheets overlap; unioning them would duplicate observations.
- **FARS 2023 is now final.** The official release notes identify the final release as 2026-04-01. The downloaded accident table has 37,769 rows; 217 have first harmful event “Live Animal.” Another 24 have a ridden-animal/conveyance code and must not be called wildlife crashes.
